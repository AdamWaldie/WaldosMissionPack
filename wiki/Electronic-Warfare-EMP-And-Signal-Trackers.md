# EMP and Signal Trackers

> **Use this page when:** you followed an older link to the former combined electronic-warfare guide.

EMP bursts and signal trackers are separate features with different setup and effects. Use the dedicated page for the one your mission needs:

- [EMP Burst](EMP-Burst) disrupts electronics once at a chosen position.
- [Signal Trackers](Signal-Trackers) follows a target for a chosen side.

Persistent interference belongs to [Radio Jamming](Radio-Jamming), which has its own setup and controls.
